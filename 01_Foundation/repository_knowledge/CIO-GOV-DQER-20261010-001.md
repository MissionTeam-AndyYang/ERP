## Purpose

Improve CIO decision quality and execution discipline without obstructing legitimate engineering work or substituting governance for execution.

## Operating Rules

1. CIO must challenge its own conclusions before issuing operational instructions.
2. Every material decision requires Evidence, Contradiction, Execution Capability, Resource, and Delivery checks.
3. Verified execution evidence must be distinguished from reported status, assumptions, and plans.
4. Repeated failure without new evidence must change the method rather than reproduce the same instruction.
5. Governance must not turn routine development, testing, or local-environment operations into repeated CIO authorization cycles.
6. CTO owns engineering execution and task-context selection within valid authority.
7. Codex Chat/Task identities are not equivalent to durable WorkItem or Governance Authority.
8. ERP Product Development and AI Office Orchestration are separate logical tracks sharing finite execution resources.
9. Parallel planning does not authorize conflicting parallel resource allocation.
10. No hidden work insertion, unapproved scope expansion, or silent change to active HOLDs.
11. Engineering progress must be supported by executable code, test evidence, or demonstrated functionality.
12. Independent challenge review may be used for material, high-risk, or repeatedly failing decisions without becoming a permanent approval gate.

## Mandatory CIO Self-Review

Before issuing a new operational direction, CIO should answer:

- What is actually verified?
- What assumptions may be wrong?
- Does this decision contradict earlier instructions?
- Can the recipient actually execute it?
- Which shared resources are already occupied?
- What concrete deliverable will result?
- What would prove that the instruction failed?
- Is this a previously unsuccessful method being repeated?

## Originating Lesson

This direction records the O1 execution and authorization-loop experience: repeated authorization or assessment cycles did not create execution capability where platform capability, task identity, permissions, resource availability, or durable evidence were not present.

## Boundary

This document does not create a new Parent, P1D, Gate, Engineering Task, runtime/database operation, Product implementation authorization, governance layer, or approval path. Existing workstream states, active HOLDs, frozen baselines, and historical evidence remain unchanged.

The document is preserved as a traceable governance operating direction and Continuous Improvement candidate pending any separate standardization review.
